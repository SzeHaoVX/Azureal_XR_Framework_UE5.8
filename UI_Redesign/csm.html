Before you pick light

Light is the better-looking one on this web page, and that is exactly the trap. In the headset your config runs r.DefaultFeature.AutoExposure=False with r.DefaultFeature.Bloom=True — a bright panel never gets adapted down, and bloom smears a near-white fill into the dark text sitting on it. At 50 cm and arm’s length in a dim substation, it is also a small lamp pointed at the trainee.

If you want light, the safe version is the one above: a mid-value frosted grey (rgba(238,238,243,0.88)), never white, with the accent darkened to #0071e3 so it still reads against a pale ground. Judge it in the headset with bloom on before committing — it is the one decision on this page a screenshot cannot settle.
